# 🧾 Olist E-Commerce Analytics Project – Business Brief

**Project Context:**

Olist is a leading Brazilian e-commerce marketplace that connects sellers with customers nationwide. While the platform has experienced rapid growth, management faces operational challenges related to customer retention, delivery efficiency, and revenue stability. This project simulates a real-world business engagement to provide actionable insights and predictive intelligence to drive strategic decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary goal is to leverage historical transaction and customer data to answer key business questions, support decision-making, and optimize revenue growth.

### Key Questions:

1. **Revenue Analysis**
   * What drives monthly revenue growth?
   * How do new versus repeat customers contribute to total revenue?
   * Are there seasonal trends or fluctuations in sales?
2. **Customer Insights**
   * Why are some customers churning?
   * Which customers are high-value or at-risk of leaving?
   * Can we segment customers into actionable groups for targeted marketing?
3. **Operational Efficiency**
   * Do delivery delays affect customer satisfaction and review scores?
   * Which regions or sellers experience the most delays?
   * How can logistics performance be improved?
4. **Predictive Analytics**
   * Can we predict future revenue based on historical patterns?
   * Can we forecast customer churn and proactively address it?
   * Can we predict late deliveries to improve operational planning?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

To track performance, the following KPIs will be calculated and monitored:

* **Revenue KPIs:** GMV (Gross Merchandise Value), Monthly Revenue, Average Order Value (AOV), Month-over-Month Growth
* **Customer KPIs:** Monthly Active Customers (MAC), Repeat Purchase Rate, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Churn Rate
* **Operational KPIs:** Delivery Delay Rate, Average Delivery Time, Review Scores by Delivery Performance
* **Predictive KPIs:** Churn Probability, Revenue Forecast, Late Delivery Probability

---

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

The project will follow a structured data analytics workflow:

1. **Stage 1 – SQL & Data Exploration:** Create a relational database from the Olist dataset, perform data quality checks, and calculate descriptive KPIs.
2. **Stage 2 – Python EDA:** Merge tables in Python, perform feature engineering, and visualize trends and correlations.
3. **Stage 3 – Predictive Modeling:** Develop models for customer churn prediction and revenue forecasting using machine learning.
4. **Stage 4 – Dashboard & Recommendations:** Present KPIs and predictions in an executive-friendly dashboard with actionable insights.

---

**Outcome:**

This project simulates an end-to-end business analytics engagement, providing insights that would guide Olist management in increasing revenue, reducing churn, and improving operational efficiency.
